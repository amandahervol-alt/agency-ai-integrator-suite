# Client AI Discovery Framework & Blueprint

> **EZMarketing AI Practice Asset**  
> Designed for leading discovery calls with small & medium-sized businesses (SMBs) to translate business bottlenecks into measurable AI solutions.

---

## 1. Discovery Call Agenda (30–45 Minutes)

1. **Introduction & Objectives (5 mins)**: Align on goals and establish an open, consultative environment.
2. **Current Workflow & Bottleneck Mapping (15 mins)**: Uncover manual tasks, delayed response times, and system silos.
3. **Data Infrastructure & Tooling Assessment (10 mins)**: Identify existing platforms (CRM, Quickbooks, Microsoft 365, Meta Ads, Google Workspace).
4. **Security, Compliance & Access Governance (5 mins)**: Address data privacy, access controls, and output safety requirements.
5. **ROI & Solution Architecture Mapping (10 mins)**: Define key performance indicators (KPIs), estimated monthly token/infra budget, and next steps.

---

## 2. Key Discovery Questions Matrix

| Focus Area | Discovery Questions | Business Impact / KPI |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Lead Capture & Response** | • How fast do inbound leads get a response today?<br>• What percentage of inquiries arrive outside business hours? | Increase lead conversion rate by 25–40%; reduce response time to < 60 seconds. |
| **Repetitive Operations** | • What manual tasks take up 5+ hours of team time per week?<br>• Where are data transfers occurring manually between spreadsheets and CRM? | Reclaim 15–20 hours/week per employee; eliminate data entry errors. |
| **System Integrations** | • Which core tools do you use daily (HubSpot, Quickbooks, Outlook)?<br>• Are APIs or webhooks currently enabled? | Seamless sync across marketing, sales, and accounting layers. |
| **Security & Guardrails** | • Are there industry privacy regulations (e.g. HIPAA, PII)?<br>• Who needs access to historical client conversation data? | Ensure zero data leaks, GDPR/CCPA compliance, and prompt security. |

## 3. Bottleneck-to-Solution Translation Template

### Scenario: Local Home Services (Plumbing / HVAC)
- **Problem**: Missed calls during evenings/weekends lead to lost service appointments.
- **Proposed AI Architecture**: 
  1. Event-driven Webhook listener on inbound call/SMS platform (Twilio/Vapi).
  2. Model Context Protocol (MCP) server connected to CRM schedule & service area database.
  3. AI Agent qualifies emergency vs. standard service requests, books open slot in schedule, and logs transaction to Quickbooks.
- **Estimated ROI**: 12–18 additional booked service calls per month (~$4,500 monthly revenue boost for ~$100/mo API infrastructure spend).
